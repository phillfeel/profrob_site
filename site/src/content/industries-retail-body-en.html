02 — PROBLEMS

Why retail needs a robot.

Fewer people are available for cleaning, for reasons a store can’t influence.

  1. > 80%

    Cleaning depends on migrant labor.

    Over 80% of cleaning staff are foreign nationals. In 2025, 11 regions introduced additional restrictions on hiring them.

  2. ×2–3

    Payroll is rising.

    In five years, cleaning companies’ payroll has grown two- to threefold. That goes into the cleaning contract price.

  3. 15%

    Frontline staff are scarce.

    15% of retailers name frontline staff shortages as their main reason to modernize.

  4. Large area, short window.

    Concourses, sales floor and parking must be washed before opening, then cleaned by day without disturbing shoppers.

03 — FREQUENCY

The sales floor is cleaned several times a day.

Each cleaning is a separate pass over the entire area. The more passes, the more sense it makes to give them to a robot and leave spot work to people.
