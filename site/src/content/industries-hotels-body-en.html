01 — HOSPITALITY

Robotics for hotels and HoReCa.

Hotels run on frontline staff who are ever harder to hire and keep. Housekeepers, waiters, floor runners: turnover hurts both service and the budget.

Robots take over the routine: room delivery, cleaning common areas and part of the front-desk work, so people can focus on guests.

02 — PROBLEMS

Where hotels lose money and service.

  1. 01

    Turnover, seasonality

    High season: not enough people. Low season: a full team is costly.

  2. 02

    Night lobby cleaning

    Cleaning must happen when guests are out, and a night crew means premiums and the same turnover.

  3. 03

    Floor-to-floor delivery

    Towels, water and room orders eat into staff time.

  4. 04

    Consistent guest experience

    Service quality shouldn’t depend on who’s on shift today.
