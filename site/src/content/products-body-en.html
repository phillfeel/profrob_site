03 — ROBOT RENTAL

Rental instead of buying. Start without big upfront costs.

Robot rental (RaaS, Robots-as-a-Service) removes the large one-off payment: a lower entry cost, a faster start, pay for use.

  • Lower entry cost

    A regular payment instead of a big purchase. No need to secure an equipment budget in advance.

  • Faster start

    You test the robot at your own site and decide on buying from real numbers.

  • Pay for use

    Take one for high season or a pilot and return it when utilization drops. We choose equipment from several brands.
