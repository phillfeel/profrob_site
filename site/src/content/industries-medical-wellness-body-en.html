01 — HEALTH & WELLNESS

Healthcare and wellness robotics.

A clinic is full of routine work that takes time away from medical staff: running lab samples and medication, disinfecting rooms, delivering linen.

Robots take over this logistics. Staff focus on patients, and hygiene procedures run to standard with nothing skipped.

Estimate staff relief Request a consultation

Audience hospitals and clinics · rehabilitation centers · wellness centers

Medical use requires checking the equipment’s certification and registration in Russia. We don’t promise medical functions without confirmed approval documents, and we select equipment in line with the facility’s requirements.
