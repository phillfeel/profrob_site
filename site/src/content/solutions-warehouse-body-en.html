01 — WAREHOUSE ROBOTICS

AMR robots and warehouse robotics.

You can buy an AMR from any supplier. The hard part comes later: robots must be connected to your WMS or 1C, spread across routes and kept online. We deploy software that manages a fleet of AMRs of different brands, and put it on robots you already have or ones we select.

06 — ECONOMICS

How to count AMR payback.

A spec sheet will not show payback: it depends on your warehouse’s routes, shifts and peaks.

What the calculation is made of

Distance walked
how many kilometers per shift people and forklifts cover on routes that can be handed to a robot
Shifts and peaks
how many shifts the warehouse runs and how much the load jumps between peak and low season; we count by the peak, not the average
Errors and damage
the share of picking errors and cargo damage before and after
Cost of deployment
robot, charging, integration with WMS or 1C, training, SLA-backed service: all in one estimate, not in parts after launch
