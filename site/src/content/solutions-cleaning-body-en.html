04 — SOFTWARE

What our software adds to any robot.

On its own a robot just drives around a map. It becomes a cleaning result when a system stands behind it.

How the platform works

  1. One fleet, one window

    Robots of different brands and classes appear on one map: status, charge, errors.

    You get: one screen instead of an app for every brand

  2. Tasks without overlaps

    The platform builds the schedule and splits the area between robots, so they do not get in each other’s way or leave gaps.

    You get: no missed zones

  3. A report of what was actually cleaned

    What was cleaned, when and how long it took. Operation history and deviations from the standard.

    You get: you see the result, not hours of work

  4. Elevators, doors, your systems

    We link the robot to building systems and your software through the platform API, so it moves between floors on its own.

    You get: fewer manual carries and requests

  5. Service and incidents

    A fault becomes a ticket automatically and the response time is recorded. Predictive maintenance reports wear in advance.

    You get: less downtime

  6. Your workflow

    Need logic that does not come in the box? We write software for your process within one platform.

    You get: built for your task, not for a catalog

How deep the integration goes depends on the access the maker gives: an open API, an SDK or only the cloud. We check this at the site assessment, before you buy.

06 — ECONOMICS

How to count payback.

Two mistakes: taking the spec-sheet speed and assuming the robot replaces the whole crew. We count more carefully.

Our assumptions

Cleaner wage
RUB 67,000 a month plus 30% employer social contributions
People freed
on one shift, no more than 2 FTEs per robot and no more than half of the team
Stays with people
30% of the floor work: corners, edges, spots
Service and consumables
8% of the robot price a year, brushes and electricity RUB 60,000 a year, platform operator share RUB 120,000 a year
Chemicals and water
40% lower use
Service life
5 years

our model, roi-model.js
