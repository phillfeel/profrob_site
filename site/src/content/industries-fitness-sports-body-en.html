01 — FITNESS & SPORTS FACILITIES

Robots for fitness clubs and sports facilities.

Russian sanitary rules require every room in a sports facility to be wet-cleaned every day, and gyms, equipment, showers and locker rooms to be disinfected too. Yet the club is open from morning to night, so cleaning has to fit in between workouts.

Robots take over the gym and restroom floors, the pool bottom, the lawn and the field markings. People handle equipment, surface disinfection and quality checks.

02 — PROBLEMS

Meeting sanitary rules with fewer people.

  1. Every room is wet-cleaned every day.

    Sports facilities must be wet-cleaned daily with detergents and disinfectants. Gyms and equipment (machines, mats), restrooms, showers, locker rooms and the first-aid room are disinfected every day.

    SP 2.1.3678-20, summarized by Rospotrebnadzor’s TsGON

  2. Fewer cleaners available.

    Over 80% of cleaning staff are foreign nationals, and cleaning companies’ payroll has grown two- to threefold in five years. In 2025, 11 regions added new limits on hiring migrant workers.

  3. Cleaning has to fit between workouts.

    Cleaning happens when the gym is almost empty: early morning, late evening or off-peak lulls.

  4. There’s more to clean than floors.

    The pool basin, stadium lawn and field markings are separate jobs, each with its own schedule and equipment.

05 — FIRST STEPS

Start with one zone.

We found no public deployments of robots in Russian fitness clubs, so we don’t show anyone else’s numbers. We propose a pilot at your site: the result will be yours.

  1. 01

    Walkthrough

    We walk the zones, measure areas and check floor surfaces, thresholds, aisles between machines and opening hours.

  2. 02

    One zone

    The gym is the easiest place to start: a large area, cleaned daily. The robot works before opening or off-peak.

  3. 03

    Review and decision

    We compare how much cleaning time the robots free up and how well cleanliness holds. Then we decide whether robots should take the locker rooms, pool or field.
