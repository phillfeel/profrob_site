05 — FAQ

Short answers to what people ask most.

Didn’t find your question? Ask an engineer, we’ll answer for your site.

Ask a question
How fast does a cleaning robot pay back?

It depends on three things: how much floor area the robot cleans, how many shifts it works and what cleaner’s wage it replaces. The payback calculator gives an estimate in a couple of minutes. We calculate the exact period during the site assessment and confirm it in a pilot.

Too expensive? Can we start without a big outlay?

Yes. Robot rental (RaaS) removes the large one-off payment: you pay for use. If you buy, we help with installments and leasing from leading Russian banks. And before any investment, you can run a pilot at your site and check the figures.

What if a robot breaks?

Service is part of the project: maintenance, diagnostics and repairs by our own service network across Russia. Response and recovery times are fixed in the SLA. The platform monitors the fleet, and AI agents predict failures before downtime.

Will we have to retrain our staff?

Staff training is part of deployment: your people learn to give the robot tasks and work with the platform. Nobody needs retraining. Staff usually move to the work the robot doesn’t do: restrooms, spot cleaning, quality control.

How do AMRs differ from AGVs?

An AGV follows a pre-marked path: magnetic tape or markers on the floor. An AMR (autonomous mobile robot) maps the space itself, plans its route and drives around obstacles. That makes an AMR easier to reconfigure when the warehouse layout changes.

What is robot rental (RaaS)?

RaaS (Robots-as-a-Service) means you pay for using a robot instead of buying it: a lower entry cost and a faster start. You can compare renting and buying on your own figures in the calculator on the Products page.

Can you automate the equipment we already have?

Often, yes. Retrofit is an upgrade of existing equipment: we add cameras, navigation, sensors and semi-automatic workflows. It can be faster and cheaper than buying a new robot.

Do you sell robots of a single brand?

No. We are not a manufacturer and are not tied to one brand. For each area we compare 2–5 manufacturers and choose those that fit the task and can be serviced in Russia.

When doesn’t robotics pay off?

Sometimes there isn’t enough work for a robot: a small area, one short shift or a task it covers only in part. Then the savings don’t cover the investment. We’ll say so plainly and offer another option: rental, an upgrade of your existing equipment or dropping the project.
