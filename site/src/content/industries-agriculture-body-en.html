01 — AGRICULTURE

Robots and drones for agriculture.

Seasonal workers are scarcer, yet fields must still be treated on time. Agricultural drones and field robots do it more precisely and faster than people.

They spray exactly where needed, save crop protection products and give you an objective picture of the field, especially where manual work can’t keep up.
