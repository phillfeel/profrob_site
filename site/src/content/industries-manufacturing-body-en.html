01 — MANUFACTURING AND INDUSTRY

Robotics for manufacturing and industrial plants.

Robotics in manufacturing doesn’t have to start with an expensive line retrofit. The fastest results come from simple things: moving materials around the shop floor, taking repetitive operations off operators, and regular equipment inspection.

That’s the place to start: payback is clearer and the investment is lower.
