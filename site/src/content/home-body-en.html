01Cleaning

Costly, inconsistent cleaning

Best for
Offices, shopping centers, warehouses, terminals, clinics: wherever floors are large, flat and hard.
Robot
Scrubs and sweeps floors along a set route, including at night and off-peak.
People
Restrooms, desks, trash, corners and areas the robot can’t reach. Water and consumables.
Site needs
Flat hard flooring, clear aisles, space for charging and draining water.

Get a preliminary estimate right away in the payback calculator. A detailed one follows a site assessment.

02Delivery

Not enough couriers

Best for
Restaurants and stores, airports, campuses, hospital grounds and industrial sites.
Robot
Carries the order in a locked compartment from pickup point to recipient at walking speed.
People
Pick and load the order, hand it to the recipient. An operator monitors the fleet remotely.
Site needs
A route along sidewalks and driveways, a loading point with charging, the site owner’s consent.

Preliminary estimate once you describe the routes and order volume.

03Cargo

The warehouse can’t keep up

Best for
Warehouses, distribution centers and production halls with constant internal transport.
Robot
Moves pallets, carts and racks between zones, or follows a worker.
People
Loading and unloading, picking, quality control and exceptions.
Site needs
Flat floor, wide enough aisles, agreed routes; integration with your warehouse system if the task needs it.

Preliminary estimate after a site assessment, based on routes, throughput and shifts.
