01 — MANUFACTURERS

Manufacturers we work with.

We work with manufacturers directly, but we are not tied to any one of them. This is deliberate: an integrator tied to one vendor sells that vendor’s equipment even where it is not the best.

Our main focus is strong Asian manufacturers: the best value for money, a wide choice and fast development. We keep several vendors in every area, so equipment can be replaced and you do not depend on a single supplier.

04 — SELECTION

How we choose equipment.

We are not tied to one vendor, so we choose equipment for your task and economics, not for our own catalog.

E.g. cleaning
For your task
  1. 01

    Task and economics

    We start with the task and calculate payback. We choose what pays back for you, not what is in stock.

  2. 02

    Service and support

    We take equipment that can actually be maintained. Our own service network across Russia, work under an SLA.

  3. 03

    Several vendors per area

    We keep 2–5 manufacturers in every area. Equipment can be replaced, and no supplier becomes the only one.

  4. 04

    One platform for all equipment

    We connect robots of different brands to the Professional Robotics platform through manufacturer adapters: one map, reports and KPIs.
