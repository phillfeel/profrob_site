02 — PROBLEMS

Why halls are hard to keep clean.

Labor shortage

> 80%

of cleaning staff in Russia are foreign nationals. Regions are also tightening rules on hiring migrants: in 2025, 11 regions added restrictions.

  1. Huge floor areas

    Waiting halls, terminals and concourses are huge, and people keep coming almost around the clock.

  2. Scheduled peaks

    Trains and flights bring people in waves. At the peak, a cleaner with a machine blocks passengers; after it, there’s the most dirt.

  3. The same questions

    Where the platforms are, which counter, how to reach the exhibition: staff spend the shift on routine answers.

03 — FLOW

Between passenger waves.

People arrive in waves. The robot washes halls at night and in lulls between peaks: it doesn’t block passengers or drive over a wet floor in a crowd.

  1. 1 00:30–05:30 Night wash Main wet cleaning of halls and concourses while traffic is at its lowest.
  2. 2 10:30–12:30 After the morning wave A pass through waiting halls after commuter trains.
  3. 3 14:00–15:45 Midday lull Spot washing of tracked-in dirt at entrances, ticket offices.
  4. 4 21:15–23:45 After the evening wave A repeat pass before night mode.
