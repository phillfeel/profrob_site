04 — HOW WE WORK

Five principles we work by.

  1. 1

    We sell results, not robots

    The conversation starts with your economics, not with equipment specifications.

  2. 2

    Independence from manufacturers

    We take equipment from those who truly fit and are serviced. Several vendors for each area, not tied to one.

  3. 3

    Engineering over sales

    If robotics won’t pay back for you, we will say so plainly. A bad project hurts you and our reputation.

  4. 4

    Service is part of the solution

    A robot without maintenance stops. We are responsible for keeping it working, not only for delivery.

  5. 5

    Every project has a measurable result

    If the effect can’t be calculated, we don’t start the project.

06 — WHY NOW

The market is only starting. This is the window.

Robotics in Russia is still at an early stage. Robot density is several times lower than in developed countries, service infrastructure is weak, and there are almost no strong integrators.

For business this is a window: what becomes the norm in a few years gives a clear advantage now to those who deploy earlier.
