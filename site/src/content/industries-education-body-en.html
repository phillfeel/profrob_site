01 — EDUCATION

Robots for schools and higher education.

Educational institutions have two different reasons to use robots. The building: hallways need cleaning every day, ideally while lessons are on. The lab: universities and colleges need humanoids and platforms for students to learn on.

These tasks have different buyers and budgets, so this page has two entry points.

02 — PROBLEMS

Why school buildings are hard to clean.

  1. 01

    Cleaning every day

    Sanitary rules for educational institutions require daily wet cleaning of all rooms.

  2. 02

    Class time is the only slot

    Hallways get dirty at breaks, and mopping among children is awkward. That leaves class time, when everyone is in class.

  3. 03

    Fewer people for cleaning

    Over 80% of cleaning staff are foreign nationals, and regions are tightening migrant hiring rules.

  4. 04

    Pricier cleaning

    Cleaning companies’ payroll has grown two- to threefold in five years, and that goes into the price of cleaning contracts.
